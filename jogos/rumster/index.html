Every path starts with a good idea.

In Rumster, move pieces of the scene to clear the route and help a small hamster reach the sunflower.

A pocket-sized adventure

Cute, clear, and increasingly challenging.

The goal is simple. The solution is not always. Each level presents a compact board where paths must fit together before the hamster can move forward.

  • Slide pieces and connect the route
  • Short levels with smart solutions
  • New obstacles introduced gradually
  • Rising difficulty without losing its lightness
